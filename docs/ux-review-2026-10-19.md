# Planner UX review · 19 October 2026

The supplied screenshots show three related issues: meaningful copy is commonly 7–11 px, secondary text fades into white surfaces, and the recipe grid reserves three columns even when the result contains one meal. Shopping conditions and ingredient quantities are particularly hard to read. A narrow basket compounds the problem by creating many short lines.

The revised direction keeps the existing warm green identity and Cyrillic font pairing, with a practical pantry workspace: parchment page `#eae9e0`, white panels, distinct borders, dark green primary actions, and clearly visible input outlines. Body text is 16 px; secondary labels and explanations are at least 14 px. Recipe titles are 20–24 px. Existing controls and tab behavior stay intact.

## Layout and interaction

- Recipe tracks use `auto-fit` so occupied cards fill the available width. A single meal becomes a wide image-and-details card on desktop and a stacked card on mobile. Empty slots are removed.
- Desktop results pair flexible recipe content with a basket at least 340 px wide. The basket sticks 24 px below the viewport top, with a viewport-bounded scroll area. At 1100 px and below it returns to normal document flow.
- Mobile navigation uses three columns and two rows with 14 px labels, replacing six compressed columns. It remains in document flow so larger text does not overlap the content.
- Inputs are at least 48 px tall; core buttons are 48 px and smaller recipe actions are 44 px. Focus rings remain visible. Color states also retain labels, icons, or text.
- Shopping source links are styled separately from the checkbox label. Source and checked dates remain legible without making a link activate the shopping checkbox.
- Metric categories keep independent accents. Optional `goal-met` and `over-goal` classes support goal outcomes. Unlimited values should not imply a percentage of a nonexistent target.
